### Full Post:

Sam Altman said something lately that we should all pay attention to:

“We see a future where intelligence is a utility, like electricity or water, and people will buy it from us on a meter and use it for whatever they want to use it for. The demand that we see for that seems to continue to go like this 📈”

Two things can be true at the same time:

1) Many of the LLMs have trained their models on the content people wrote, created and shared for free and they're now selling it back to the people. Messy. 

2) People are using these models to make personalized, sophisticated, useful, complex and important decisions. From SaaS products to personal health decisions... People are using these tools daily to make major life choices. 

And marketers....

Marketers need to wake up. 

The content you publish today will be the training data for models tomorrow. 

The publications you acquire will be the training data for models tomorrow. 

The communities you post in will make up the training data tomorrow. 

There are two internets. 

The one humans browse. 
The one robots browse. 

It's time for marketers to shift their strategies accordingly.

## Key Themes:

### Full Post:

The most important factor for AI Visibility in 2026 👉🏿 🚨 EEAT SIGNALS 🚨

Hear me out: 

-> When a user submits a question to an LLM... The LLM modifies that question (query fan out) with a wide range of different queries, words & phrases. It looks for evidence that the information it's citing is valid and worth trusting. 

-> Brands who have spent the last few years ignoring the desire to publish 20,000 blog posts in a single month... and instead focused on building the most expert driven, research backed, high quality content possible are being cited by the LLMs at a higher rate than ever before. 

-> Brands who have done this on platforms OTHER than their own site are winning by an even BIGGER margin. In a recent study that we did with AirOps across B2B SaaS; we found that more than 80% of a categories high priority keywords in LLMs resulted in citations OUTSIDE of their own domain

Why?

Because the LLMs are using a large surface area to build a case. 

And smart brands...

Smart marketing teams...

And smart chief marketing officers...

Are ensuring that they are spreading their stories beyond just their site. 

Remember:

Different LLMs prefer different sites. 

We did one study and found that Perplexity loves LinkedIn. 

We did another study and found that Gemini loves YouTube. (duh) 

And we did another that found that ChatGPT loves Reddit. 

You have to run your own analysis. 

You have to run your own reports. 

And then...

You have to run a marketing playbook rooted in EEAT that will help you win.
