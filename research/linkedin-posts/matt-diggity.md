### Full Post:

I tested dozens of AI SEO tactics and found 7 that actually work.

Implemented on a client site, these strategies drove 2300% more AI traffic… 

And got them appearing in 90 keywords in AI Overviews (from 0):

#1: TARGET CONVERSATIONAL QUERIES

Traditional keywords don't trigger AI Overviews like conversational queries do.

Traditional: "15-minute home workout" (no AI Overview)
Conversational: "What's a quick 15-minute workout I can do at home without equipment?" (triggers AI Overview)

Use Google's "People Also Ask" section to find these conversational queries.

Add these exact questions as H2s, then answer them directly.

#2: STRUCTURE WITH CLEAR HEADINGS

AI loves content it can easily scan and understand.

Use this exact heading hierarchy:

- ONE H1 that states the topic clearly
- H2s for major sections/questions
- H3s for sub-points within sections

#3: BE DIRECT & CONCISE

AI will ignore #1 ranking sites in favor of lower-ranking pages with clearer writing.

Vague, metaphorical language entertains humans but confuses AI. 

Direct, factual statements get featured in AI Overviews.

#4: ADD SUMMARY SECTIONS FOR EASY EXTRACTION

AI loves content that summarizes key points upfront.

Add either:

- A brief TL;DR summary (2-3 sentences)
- A bullet-point "Key Takeaways" section

Place them at the beginning of articles. 

This makes it easy for AI to extract your main points without crawling the entire page.

#5: MONITOR YOUR BRAND ACROSS AI PLATFORMS

AI often describes your brand differently than you'd expect.

Use these exact prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot:

"What can you tell me about [your brand]?"
"What does [your brand] do?"

Check if the descriptions are accurate.

If you find discrepancies, fix them by:

- Updating your About Us and FAQ pages
- Claiming and updating Google Business Profile, Yelp, LinkedIn and Trustpilot listings
- Making your brand description consistent across all platforms

AI pulls from these sources directly to form its opinion of you.

#6: DEMONSTRATE AUTHORITY WITH REAL EXPERTISE

AI prioritizes content from trusted sources.

Add these specific trust signals:

- Expert author bios with credentials
- Case studies with real data
- Industry certifications displayed prominently
- Citations from reputable sources

#7: COLLECT AND SHOWCASE REVIEWS

We did a single-variable test on a law firm site stuck in rankings for months. The ONLY change we made was adding Google Reviews.

Result: Immediate ranking improvements in both traditional AND AI search.

Display reviews prominently on your site.

Then actively engage with all user content:

- Respond to comments (even negative ones)
- Answer questions on forums
- Engage with social mentions

AI sees this as proof of legitimacy.

## Insights:

* Conversational queries are more likely to trigger AI Overviews.
* Content structure and clarity matter more than rankings alone.
* AI pulls brand information from multiple sources, making consistency essential.
* Expertise and trust signals increase AI visibility.
* Reviews and user engagement can improve both traditional and AI search performance.
