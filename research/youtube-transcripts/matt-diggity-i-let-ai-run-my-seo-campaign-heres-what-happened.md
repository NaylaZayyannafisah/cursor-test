## 1. Topical Authority Through Content Scale

### The Principle

Google prefers websites that demonstrate comprehensive coverage within a niche. Rather than ranking isolated articles, Google tends to rely on domains it perceives as authorities.

### The Strategy

1. Perform competitor analysis.
2. Identify content gaps.
3. Export uncovered topics and keywords.
4. Build a content production pipeline to systematically fill those gaps.

### Goal

Produce more high-quality topical content than competitors to strengthen topical authority signals.

## 4. Two-Step Content Generation Framework

Matt emphasizes that AI content performs better when generated in two stages.

### Stage 1: Outline Creation

Prompt AI to create:

* Introduction
* Main sections
* Instructions
* Supporting topics

Output:

Structured article framework.

---

### Stage 2: Content Expansion

Feed the outline back into the model and instruct it to:

* Expand sections.
* Follow tone requirements.
* Include keywords naturally.
* Maintain formatting.
* Optimize readability.

### Key Principle

> Outline first, content second.

Separating planning from writing consistently produces better outputs than generating full articles in one prompt.

## 📝 Strategic Summary

Matt Diggity presents AI SEO not as a replacement for SEO strategy, but as a force multiplier for execution. The core idea is transforming repetitive SEO work into automated workflows using no-code tools like Make.com combined with OpenAI.

His system starts with competitor gap analysis and topic extraction. Those topics are fed into Google Sheets, where Make.com orchestrates interactions with OpenAI. Rather than generating articles directly, the workflow first creates outlines and then expands them into full content, producing more structured and higher-quality pages.

Matt advocates a content portfolio approach. Low-value informational pages can be produced at scale through automation to build topical authority, while revenue-driving pages should receive human attention or utilize more advanced AI solutions.

The same architecture extends beyond article writing. Metadata creation, title generation, and outreach campaigns can all be automated, allowing SEO teams to eliminate repetitive tasks and focus on strategy rather than production.

Ultimately, AI becomes an operations layer rather than merely a writing assistant. The leverage comes not from better prompts, but from building systems that execute hundreds of tasks simultaneously.
